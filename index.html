Japan Toolkit

A small collection of utilities for navigating life in Japan.

Era Date Converter

Convert between Gregorian dates and Japanese era dates (Reiwa, Heisei, Showa, Taisho, Meiji).

Enter a 7-digit Japanese postal code to look up the prefecture, city, and town — editable fields so you can correct the auto-fill.

PR Points Calculator

Estimate your Highly Skilled Foreign Professional points toward Japan Permanent Residency.
