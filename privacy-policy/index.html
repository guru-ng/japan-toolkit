Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Japan Toolkit ("this site") provides free utilities — an era date converter, a postal code lookup, and a PR points estimator. This page explains what data is collected when you use the site.

Information we collect

The tools on this site run entirely in your browser. Dates, postal codes, and calculator inputs you enter are not sent to or stored on any server operated by this site — they exist only in your browser's memory for the current session.

The Postal Code Lookup tool sends the postal code you enter to the third-party ZipCloud API to retrieve address data. See ZipCloud's own terms for how they handle that request.

Cookies & advertising

This site may display advertising served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or www.aboutads.info.

Analytics

This site may use privacy-focused analytics to understand aggregate traffic (e.g. page views per tool). Any such analytics do not identify individual users.
